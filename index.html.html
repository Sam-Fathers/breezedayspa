About Breeze

A Calm Escape in Deloraine

Breeze Day Spa is located in the picturesque town of Deloraine, in Tasmania's Meander Valley. We offer a wide range of massage and spa treatments, from relaxing full-body massages to restorative facials, each one designed to help you slow down and feel like yourself again.

Every treatment uses beautiful artisan-made products from Waterlily, proudly and completely Australian made. Our ethos at Breeze is simple: to provide a calm, relaxing environment where you can escape your everyday stresses and take a little time for yourself.
