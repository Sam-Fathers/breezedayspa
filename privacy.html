Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Breeze Day Spa ("we") respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect when you request a booking through our website, why, and how we look after it.

What we collect

  • Your first name and phone number
  • The treatment, session length, date and time you'd like
  • Anything you choose to tell us in the notes, which may include health information such as medical conditions, allergies, pregnancy or skin sensitivities

Why we collect it

We use these details only to contact you about your booking, arrange your appointment, and provide your treatment safely. We ask you to agree to this before you send the form.

How it reaches us

When you send the booking form, your details are delivered to our business email inbox by Web3Forms (web3forms.com), a form delivery service. Web3Forms handles your details only to deliver them to us.

Where we keep it, and who sees it

Your details are kept only in our business email inbox. We don't enter them into any other system, and we don't sell or share them with anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

We keep booking emails only as long as we need them to look after you, then delete them.

Seeing, correcting or deleting your details

You can ask to see the details we hold about you, correct them, or have them deleted. Email hello@breezedayspa.com.au or call 0456 148 459.

Our website

Our website doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Like any website, our host (Cloudflare) and the services that supply our fonts (Google Fonts) and some photos (Pexels) receive your device's IP address when they send content to your browser.

Questions or complaints

If you have a question or complaint about your privacy, please contact us first using the details above and we'll respond as soon as we can. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
